Mission

Who we are

We are a community of heritage Americans committed to preserving, protecting, and celebrating the history, traditions, and cultural character of Appalachia and the American people who built their lives here. We believe Appalachia is more than a place on a map—it is a living inheritance of family, faith, music, craftsmanship, land, language, and memory. Much of that heritage is increasingly threatened by cultural erasure, neglect, and forces that would diminish the traditions that give our communities their identity. Our mission is to keep that inheritance alive: to honor the people who came before us, preserve the places and traditions they left behind, and ensure that future generations can know, understand, and take pride in the culture that shaped them.

History & Culture

What we come from

Appalachia's story runs through coal camps and historical battlefields, and through generations who contributed unimaginable amounts to the nation, even before it existed. From the Revolution to the Civil War, people from our region are quite literally the "underdogs." Would America be the same had Virginia not existed?

It runs through music, as well, fiddle and banjo tunes carried over by Scots-Irish and Anglo-Saxon settlers, reshaped in these hollows into something distinctly ours, and passed down at church gatherings long before it had a name like "bluegrass."

Where We Stand

Our positions

Appalachian Vanguard takes positions on many of the issues that most directly affect our communities. Below are just a few of our stances.

Gun Rights: We are entirely Pro-2A, with zero exceptions except for laws limiting non-citizens. We reject Virginia Governor Spanberger's unconstitutional gun control bills.

Religion: We are a Christian organization and view it as both a building block for Western civilization and the United States. We believe America is a Christian nation. Christian belief isn't required for membership, but accepting that we want to spread the Gospel is.

“Blessed is the nation whose God is the Lord...” Psalm 33:12

Immigration: Immigration is one of the most important points of debate, not only in modern politics, but also throughout history. Without going into too much detail, we believe strongly in a nation's ability to determine who enters the country, and we consider ourselves immigration restrictionists.

Foreign Policy: Although we would hesitate to label ourselves as isolationist, we believe in a true America First foreign policy and in the Monroe Doctrine. We should focus on improving the lives of Americans before we try world policing with nations abroad, and we would support bills to prevent dual citizens from holding public office. We support ending all aid to Israel especially, and believe that AIPAC and its aggressive lobbying needs to be outlawed.

FAQ

Common questions

Is Appalachian Vanguard a registered nonprofit?+
We are not, and we do not have plans to do so.
Where does Appalachian Vanguard operate?+
We're based in Southwest Virginia.
How can I get involved?+
Direct membership is limited for now, but you can submit an application on the join page.
Why doesn't Appalachian Vanguard name its members or leadership?+
We keep the focus on the region rather than any individual. It also protects the privacy and safety of members who are still building this alongside other jobs and commitments.
Is my email kept private if I contact you?+
Yes. Messages come to a dedicated, private email account and are not shared, sold, or published.
Can I donate?+
Not at this time, but we greatly appreciate the interest.
